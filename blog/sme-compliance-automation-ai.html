Choosing the Right Tool

Look for these features:

  • India-specific: Built for GST, Indian labor laws, not just US tax
  • Integration: Connects with Tally, Zoho, QuickBooks
  • Multi-compliance: Handles GST + TDS + PF/ESI, not just one
  • Alerts: Proactive deadline and risk notifications
  • Audit trail: Document everything for inspections
  • Support: Human help when AI can't figure something out

What AI Can't Do (Yet)

Be realistic about limitations:

  • Complex legal interpretations still need lawyers
  • Novel situations without precedent
  • Negotiations with authorities
  • Strategic tax planning

AI handles the 80% routine work, freeing your CA/lawyer for the 20% that needs human judgment.
