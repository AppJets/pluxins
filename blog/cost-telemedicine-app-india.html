Compliance & Legal Costs

Healthcare apps in India must comply with several regulations:

  • Telemedicine Practice Guidelines (MCI): Your platform must follow these. Legal review costs ₹1-3 lakhs.
  • Data Protection: DPDP Act compliance, data encryption, consent management. ₹2-5 lakhs.
  • HIPAA (if serving US patients): Additional ₹5-15 lakhs for compliance.
  • Company Registration & Licenses: ₹50K-2 lakhs depending on structure.

Cost-Saving Tips

  1. Start with MVP: Launch with video calls + booking. Add features based on user feedback.
  2. Use Cross-Platform: React Native/Flutter saves 30-40% vs native iOS + Android.
  3. Video API Choice: 100ms and Dyte are Indian alternatives, often cheaper than Twilio.
  4. Outsource Smartly: Use Indian dev agencies (₹1500-3000/hr) vs US ($100-200/hr).
  5. Open Source: Use Jitsi for video (free) if budget is tight.

Should You Build or Buy?

Build Custom If:

  • • You need unique workflows
  • • Integration with existing hospital systems
  • • Long-term competitive advantage
  • • Budget > ₹30 lakhs

Use White-Label If:

  • • Speed to market is critical
  • • Standard telemedicine features suffice
  • • Budget < ₹15 lakhs
  • • Testing market before full investment
