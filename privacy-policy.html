Last updated: September 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Pluxins ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit our website, contact us, or use services delivered by Pluxins or its subsidiaries.

We use the information collected to respond to inquiries, prepare proposals, deliver contracted services, process listing or course orders, send project updates, and improve our website and offerings. We do not sell your personal information.

Our website may use cookies and third-party analytics tools to understand how visitors use the site. You can disable cookies through your browser settings; some site features may not function as intended if you do.

These providers process data under their own privacy policies. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the information you share with us. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We retain project and contact information for as long as needed to fulfil our services, comply with legal obligations, and maintain business records, after which it is deleted or anonymised.

7. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information held by us at any time by contacting us using the details below.

8. Children's Privacy

Our website and consulting services are not directed at children under 13. Our EdTech subsidiary, ClassGini, handles student data under its own privacy policy designed for educational use.
